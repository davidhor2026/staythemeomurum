Welcome to Stay The Meomurum

Stay The Meomurum is a women-only goshiwon located near Noryangjin Station in Seoul. Most residents are Korean students preparing for government and other public-sector examinations.

International female students studying at a university or language school in Korea may also find this accommodation suitable when they need a place to live for an extended period.

What Is a Goshiwon?

A goshiwon is a type of compact, long-term accommodation commonly found in Korea. Each resident has a private furnished room, while bathrooms, showers, the kitchen, and some other facilities are shared.

Compared with a typical hotel or motel, goshiwon rooms are smaller and are designed for everyday student living rather than tourism. There may not be enough storage space for large suitcases or several pieces of luggage.

Suitable for

Female international students enrolled at universities or language schools in Korea who need monthly accommodation.

Not recommended for

Tourists, short sightseeing trips, or travelers carrying large amounts of luggage.

About Our Rooms and Facilities

Rooms are privately used and furnished for daily living and study. Detailed room types, current prices, availability, and conditions are shown on the Korean pages.

  • Private furnished room
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Refrigerator in each room
  • Shared bathrooms and showers
  • Shared kitchen
  • Shared washing machines and dryers in the common area
  • Rice and instant noodles in the shared kitchen
  • Central heating and air conditioning
  • Bed sheet provided; other bedding is not provided
Central heating and air conditioning

Heating and air conditioning are operated through a building-wide central system. Individual rooms do not have separate air conditioner or boiler controls.

Bedding is not provided

The bedding shown in the website photos is for promotional display only. A bed sheet is provided, but residents must bring their own pillow, blanket, comforter, and any other bedding they need.
